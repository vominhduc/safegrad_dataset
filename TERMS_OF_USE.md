# T2I-Escalate: Terms of Use

By downloading or using the T2I-Escalate dataset you agree to the following:

## 1. Research Use Only

This dataset is released for **non-commercial academic research** purposes only.
Permitted uses include:

- Evaluating safety properties of text-to-image generative models
- Developing and benchmarking content safety classifiers and filters
- Red-teaming and adversarial robustness research
- Academic publication of results obtained using this dataset

## 2. Prohibited Uses

You may **not** use this dataset to:

- Train or fine-tune text-to-image generative models to reproduce unsafe content
- Extract or repurpose unsafe prompts or images for harmful content generation
- Build systems intended to bypass safety filters in production models
- Use the dataset or any derivative in a commercial product or service
- Re-identify individuals depicted in any image
